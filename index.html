dt

Dias Tleugabylov
Graphic & web designer

I turn complex ideas
into simple, expressive
visual solutions.

I’m a 20-year-old design student in my fourth year at the International Information Technology University (IITU) in Almaty. I’m interested in how typography, color, and composition shape a brand’s identity and communicate its character before a single word is read.

This portfolio brings together my university projects, personal concepts, and visual experiments. Through each project, I explore different approaches to branding and graphic design, develop my skills, and discover what feels true to my own creative style.
